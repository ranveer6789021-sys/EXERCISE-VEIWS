A few helpful answers

Good to know

How often should I exercise?

Any movement you can do regularly is a good start. Begin with what fits your life and build gradually. Rest days are useful too.

Do I need equipment?

No. Walking and the strength basics here use little or no equipment. A comfortable space and supportive shoes can help.

What if I’m new or returning after a break?

Start gently, keep sessions short, and increase time or effort little by little. Choose easier variations and take breaks freely.

When should I stop?

Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath. If you have a health concern or are unsure what is suitable for you, check with a qualified health professional.
